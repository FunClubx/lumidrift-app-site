Thank you for using Koi Pond: Relaxing Fish. If you have questions, encounter a problem, or would like to send feedback, please contact us using the information below.

Contact Support

Please include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, and a brief description of the issue. Screenshots are also helpful when available.

Common Questions

The app is not running as expected.
Please make sure your device is using the latest compatible version of iOS or iPadOS, then fully close and reopen the app.

Audio is not playing.
Check your device volume and sound settings, and make sure audio is enabled inside the app.

I have feedback or a feature request.
We welcome suggestions. Please email us and include “Koi Pond Feedback” in the subject line.

About the App

Koi Pond: Relaxing Fish is a calming interactive koi pond experience designed for iPhone and iPad. Enjoy beautiful koi, water interaction, feeding, and a peaceful visual atmosphere.
